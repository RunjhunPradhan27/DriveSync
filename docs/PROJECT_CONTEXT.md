# DriveSync

DriveSync is a Cloud-Based Automobile Sales & Service Management Platform.

The purpose of this project is to digitize the complete workflow of an automobile dealership.

The application manages:

- Vehicle Inventory
- Customer Management
- Lead Management
- Test Drive Scheduling
- Vehicle Sales
- Service Booking
- Repair Tracking
- Spare Parts Inventory
- Technician Assignment
- Customer Feedback
- Business Analytics
